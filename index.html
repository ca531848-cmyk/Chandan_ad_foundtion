In Loving Memory

In Memory of Abhay Lutra Ji

Abhay Lutra Ji was a loving son and a very special person to his family. He shared a deep and beautiful bond with his mother. He was suffering from cancer in his leg, and sadly, because of his illness, he passed away.

His loss was a very painful moment for his family and everyone who knew him. Although Abhay Lutra Ji is no longer with us, his memories will always remain close to the hearts of his loved ones.

In his loving memory, Ad Foundation was started as a free initiative to help people and serve the community. This foundation is a small effort to keep his memory alive and to give his life a meaningful and lasting tribute.

Abhay Lutra Ji will always be remembered with love, respect, and heartfelt tribute.

🕯️ Forever remembered with love, respect and gratitude. 🕯️
